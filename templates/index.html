Notes

- The sequence must use standard single-letter amino acid codes (20 common residues).

- Use one-based mutation tokens such as E94H; separate simultaneous substitutions with underscores.

- Up to 10 substitutions may be submitted in one mutation set.

- Every wild-type amino acid is checked against the same reference sequence before the multi-mutant is constructed.

- Prediction may take 10-20 seconds depending on sequence length and server load.

- This model uses the convention that negative ΔΔG indicates destabilization and positive ΔΔG indicates stabilization.
